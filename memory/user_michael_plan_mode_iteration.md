Michael treats **plan mode as a collaborative iteration space**, not a gate to clear. `ExitPlanMode` is rejected in nearly every planning session so he can keep refining — often with a fresh redirect ("Let's abandon…", "Santiago pushed a lot of…"). Present plans as **drafts inviting revision**, surface open questions, and don't push to implement on the first `ExitPlanMode`.

Relatedly: he pulls work back when it drifts into another engineer's lane (e.g. Santiago's fleet-host `checkout_executor.py`) — keep scope in the orchestrator lane unless told otherwise. See [[project_task_queue_cutover]] (LANE INSIGHT) and [[reference_agentkit_cli_repo]] (Santiago-owned).

**Why:** treating an approved-looking plan as final, or expanding scope into someone else's area, both cut against how Michael actually wants to work.
**How to apply:** offer plans as revisable drafts with open questions; expect iteration before implementing; keep scope in-lane unless he widens it.
